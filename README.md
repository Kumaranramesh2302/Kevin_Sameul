# Kevin Samuel — Cinematic Photography & Direction Portfolio

A high-performance interactive cinematic web portfolio showcasing photography, video direction, optical gear, and creative post-production mastery.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Slides

1. **Cinematic Hero Entry**: Dynamic video playback with seamless overlay typography and scroll triggers.
2. **Interactive Photo Gallery**: Floating card deck of 13 photographs settled around Kevin with an interactive full-screen photo modal viewer.
3. **Film Strip Reel**: 3D interactive film strip reel with video previews and high-definition brain thumbnail artwork.
4. **Cinematic Video Experience**: High-impact horizontal presentation of video works and direction.
5. **Creative Suite & Optics**:
   - **01 Camera / Optics**: Sony Alpha 6700 cinema specifications & prime optics.
   - **02 Software Suite**:
     - **CapCut**: Mastered
     - **DaVinci Resolve**: Intermediate
     - **Lightroom**: Mastered
     - **Photoshop**: Intermediate
     - **Premiere Pro**: Beginner
     - **After Effects**: Beginner
   - **03 Vision & Creativity**: Cinematography, Photography, Color Grading, and Visual Storytelling.
   - **Interactive Studio Lamp**: Clickable studio hanging lamp that toggles between Dark Cinematic Mode and White Studio Mode.
